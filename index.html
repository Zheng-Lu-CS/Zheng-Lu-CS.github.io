Reinforcement Learning · CPR

Better Policies from Short Comparisons: Minimal Post-Training with Frozen World Models

Zheng Lu, Rui Huang, Lingbei Meng, Tinghao Wang, Bofan Zhu, Bowen Han, Yuxi Lu, Yiran Li, Peize He, Yaodi Luo, Zhengbo Jiao, Lian Zhang, Jianqing Zhu

Submitted to AAAI 2027

Paper (PDF)

Comparative Policy Repair (CPR) improves policies by comparing short action branches in a frozen world model and distilling relative advantages under a KL constraint. It requires no additional environment interaction, achieving 3.8%–5.2% aggregate gains on Atari and DMC and a 5.0 percentage-point gain on MT50 with only 0.1%–0.7% of the original training compute.
Multimodal · TOPS

TOPS: First-Principles Visual Token Pruning via Constructing Token Optimal Preservation Sets for Efficient MLLM Inference

Tinghao Wang*, Yichen Guo*, Rui Huang*†, Junchi Yao, Qizhe Zhang, Zheng Lu, Yuan Zhang, Jiajun Cao, Zhirong Shen, Yaosong Du, Lijie Hu, Shanghang Zhang‡

Submitted to EMNLP 2026

Paper (PDF) / arXiv

A training-free, model-agnostic visual token pruning method grounded in task relevance, information coverage, and semantic diversity. Evaluated on 7 MLLM backbones and 14 benchmarks, TOPS removes 77.8% of visual tokens while retaining 100.0% and 100.6% of baseline performance on LLaVA-NeXT-7B and 13B, respectively.
Agentic · Let It Go

Let It Go: Narrative Lock-in in Open-ended Real-Life AI Agents

Bofan Zhu, Rui Huang, Zhirong Shen, Chang Zou, Xingyu Yue, Zheng Lu, Xin Ma, Chenhang Cui, Linfeng Zhang

FAGEN Workshop @ ICML 2026 · Poster

Paper (PDF)

We characterize Narrative Lock-in: early erroneous beliefs become persistent task state and resist contradictory evidence in long-horizon agents. Across eight task domains and four models, we study this failure mode and introduce Nexus, an external oversight framework that separates execution from supervision to reduce error-path persistence and improve recovery.
